# 🏥 Health360 – Comprehensive Healthcare Risk & Disease Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

**Health360** is a healthcare data analytics project designed to explore health conditions, disease patterns, and potential risk factors through interactive data visualizations.

The project uses **Tableau dashboards** to analyze healthcare data and present meaningful insights into conditions such as heart disease, diabetes, and stroke, along with demographic and lifestyle-related factors.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze the distribution of major diseases.
- Explore health patterns across different age groups and genders.
- Examine health indicators such as blood pressure, cholesterol, and BMI.
- Visualize health risk scores and lifestyle-related factors.
- Build an interactive dashboard to make healthcare data easier to understand.
- Present insights through a web-integrated dashboard.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The Health360 dashboard includes visualizations covering:

- Gender Distribution
- Heart Disease Distribution
- Diabetes Distribution
- Stroke Distribution
- Health Risk Score Distribution
- Blood Pressure Distribution
- Cholesterol Distribution
- Age Distribution
- BMI Distribution
- Exercise and Physical Activity
- Fatigue Distribution
- Glucose Distribution
- Smoking Distribution
- Alcohol Consumption
- Diabetes Distribution Analysis
- Stroke Rate by Age
- Blood Pressure vs Heart Disease
- BMI vs Diabetes

Additional worksheets and analyses may be included in the Tableau workbook.

## 🔗 Dashboard Integration

The dashboard can be embedded into the website using Tableau's Embedding API.

Example:

```html
<script
  type="module"
  src="https://public.tableau.com/javascripts/api/tableau.embedding.3.latest.min.js">
</script>

<tableau-viz
  src="YOUR_PUBLISHED_TABLEAU_VIEW_URL"
  toolbar="bottom"
  hide-tabs
  width="100%"
  height="800">
</tableau-viz>
```

Replace `YOUR_PUBLISHED_TABLEAU_VIEW_URL` with the URL of your published Tableau view.

**Note:** If you publish the dashboard publicly, the visualization and its underlying accessible data may be visible to others. Do not publish confidential or personally identifiable health information.
